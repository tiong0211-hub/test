Economies of Scale: Why Bigger Gets Cheaper — Until It Doesn't

Spread a fixed cost over more units and the cost per unit falls, until coordination overhead and bottlenecks push it back up. Biology found the same curve: a mouse burns far more energy per gram than an elephant (metabolic rate scales as mass^¾), yet no animal grows without limit — the square–cube law eventually starves the middle.
