Allosteric Regulation: Feedback Without Touching the Active Site

Allosteric regulation lets a molecule change an enzyme's activity by binding a completely different site than the one where the reaction actually happens, twisting the enzyme's shape just enough to speed it up or shut it down.

It's the same logic behind feedback control in a plant: a downstream sensor — a composition analyzer, a discharge pressure transmitter — throttles an upstream valve or compressor without ever touching the reaction itself, using the process's own output to govern its input.

알로스테릭 조절은 어떤 분자가 반응이 실제로 일어나는 활성부위가 아닌 완전히 다른 자리에 결합해서 효소의 활성을 바꾸는 방식입니다. 그 결합이 효소의 모양을 살짝 비틀어서 반응을 빠르게 하거나 멈추게 만듭니다.
