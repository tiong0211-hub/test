Social Proof and Conformity

When we're unsure what to do, we treat other people's behavior as evidence — and the more people act, the more convincing it looks.

Physics knows this shape as a phase transition: almost nothing happens as you add more, then suddenly everything does. In a 1969 street experiment, a lone person staring at a window stopped 4% of passersby. Fifteen people stopped 40% — same sky, same street, only the crowd changed.
