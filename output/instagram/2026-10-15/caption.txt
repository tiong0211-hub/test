Distillation: Small Edges, Stacked

Vapor above a boiling ethanol–water mix is only slightly richer in ethanol than the liquid beneath it. A column repeats that tiny sorting step on tray after tray, and reflux sends liquid back down so every stage gets another pass. The trade-off: more stages or more reflux means purer product, but more capital and more energy.

The same logic drives natural selection. A few percent of advantage per generation is invisible once and unstoppable when the same filter is applied again and again.

One topic, a different lens, every morning.
