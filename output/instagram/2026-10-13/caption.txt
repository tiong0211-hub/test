Friction & Lubrication: The Stribeck Regimes

Speed up a sliding surface and oil gets dragged into the gap, lifting the two metals apart until friction drops by more than an order of magnitude. Your knees run on the same trick: synovial fluid only forms a protective film once you're moving, which is why joints feel stiff after sitting and loosen up after a few steps.

Boundary, mixed, fluid film: the Stribeck curve maps how friction falls as speed and oil viscosity rise or load eases. Design bearings to live in the fluid-film regime and wear nearly disappears.
