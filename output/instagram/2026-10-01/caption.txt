The Glass Transition: When Plastic Goes Soft

A polymer doesn't melt like ice — at its glass transition temperature the tangled chains suddenly gain enough room to wriggle, and a hard, brittle solid turns leathery over just a few degrees. Nothing crystallizes or boils; the material is the same, but its stiffness can drop by a factor of a thousand.

That's why a gasket, pipe liner or PVC fitting can be perfectly fine at 20°C and quietly creep or deform once service temperature approaches its Tg. Datasheet "max temperature" is often really a statement about this one number.
