Quantum Entanglement: Linked, Not Talking

Measure one of two entangled particles and its partner's result lines up with yours, however far apart they are. Yet each side sees only random outcomes, so no information travels: the correlation shows up only when the two records are brought together and compared.

It's the logic of a double-blind trial. The pattern is in the data from the start, but neither side can see it until the labels are unblinded.
