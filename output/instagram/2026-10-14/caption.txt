How a Habit Becomes Automatic

A habit is a cue–routine–reward loop that, with repetition, shifts control from the deliberate prefrontal cortex to the basal ganglia, until the behavior runs without a decision.

Think of a desire path across a lawn: every walker wears the groove a little deeper, and each new walker is more likely to take it — the same way erosion carves a gully from a trickle. In one well-known study, reaching automaticity took a median of 66 days, but anywhere from 18 to 254 — so missing a day barely matters; the groove is what counts.
